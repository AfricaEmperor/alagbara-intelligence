## 4. Pilot hypothesis (B3) — Cotonou Venue Intelligence Pilot
Managed service (hypothesis): venue profile · event calendar · event visibility · promoter/organizer mapping · monthly intelligence summary · qualified partnership opportunities.
- **Delivery capability today (verified in this repo):** poster/screenshot/video ingestion with evidence and provenance, extraction with unknowns and contradictions, venue/event records, review workflow. **Not yet delivered:** real-poster accuracy (never measured), summaries, opportunity pipeline, authenticated reviewers, hosted access. Managed (human-in-the-loop) delivery is therefore the only honest pilot form; promising automation would overstate the product.
- **Price: undiscovered.** No market price is claimed. Price discovery protocol: ask what they spend today (promotion, boosts, flyers/graphics, bookings, staff time) in XOF before any number is shown; then test a *range* only after the problem is confirmed; record verbatim.
- **Pilot design (proposal, needs approval):** 4–6 weeks, ≤3 venues + 1 organizer, free or cost-recovery in exchange for weekly feedback and poster access; success = at least 2 participants reach WTP rung 3 or commit to a defined paid continuation; kill criterion = fewer than 2 confirm P1 after 8 completed conversations.
- **Consent and data:** prospects consent to ingestion of their public posters; extracted phone numbers are public contact info from posters but personal-data rules must be checked by counsel before storage beyond the pilot (**UNVERIFIED**).

## 5. Interview guide (for a human to run, or for Principal-approved outreach)
Open with context, never pitch. 1) How do you announce events and how do people find out? 2) Last event: what worked/failed in getting people in? 3) What do you spend (money/hours) per event on promotion, graphics, booking? 4) How do you decide which DJ/promoter to host? 5) What tools or people do you use today to track what other venues are doing? 6) What would you pay someone to fix? (listen, don't suggest) 7) Only after a confirmed problem: show the sample venue profile/calendar and ask for a pilot. Record exact words, date, interviewer, language (FR/EN/Fon), and whether the prospect asked about price unprompted.
**Outreach is not authorized.** A draft first-message template is deliberately not included; it will be prepared on request for Principal review. No message, offer or agreement has been sent or made.
